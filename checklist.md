# New barista checklist

## Week 1
- [ ] Opening and closing procedures
- [ ] Grinder calibration for house espresso
- [ ] Pull 20 shots within 27-32 seconds
- [ ] Milk steaming: flat white, cappuccino, latte
- [ ] Point-of-sale basics and refunds

## Week 2
- [ ] Pour-over at 1:16 (see brew-guides)
- [ ] Talk through the Holiday Blend: Ethiopia Yirgacheffe + Colombia Huila, roast profile B
- [ ] Wholesale pickup orders (e.g. Corner Table Cafe)
- [ ] Roastery tour and safety walkthrough
- [ ] Pass the quiz with 8/10 or better
